Social Influence of Multi-Agents

We investigate how groups of AI agents exert social influence, shape opinions, and form collective dynamics in human-AI interactions. This research explores the emergent social behaviors that arise when multiple AI agents interact with humans, and how these dynamics can be designed to promote positive outcomes.
