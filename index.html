I am “Holly” Tianqi Song (she/her), a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore (NUS), advised by Prof. Yi-Chieh (EJ) Lee . I work in human–computer interaction (HCI), with a particular focus on how humans and AI systems co-exist, interact, and mutually influence one another in increasingly social and intelligent environments. More >

Research Interests:

  • Pillar 1: Human-AI Sociology: Investigating social mechanisms (e.g., social influence, social comparison) within interactions between humans and groups of AI agents, and designing AI-driven social simulation interventions to model social environments ("social vibes") and positively shape human attitudes and behaviors.
  • Pillar 2: Lifespan, Aging, and Temporality: Exploring whether AI systems can understand concepts of time, lifespan, and mortality, and examining how AI can provide tailored support for humans across different life stages, from youth to aging.

If you are interested in research collaboration, feel free to contact me. More >
(Note: If you are an undergraduate or master's student interested in research internships, please contact my supervisor EJ first!)

Prior to my PhD, I earned my master's degree from NUS and my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University, along with an Honors Program in Humanities and Social Sciences at the Chu Kochen Honors College .
